Sprint dates: July 27, 2026 - August 2, 2026

## Sprint Goal

Make the live storefront more premium, shoppable, and marketing-ready before scaling traffic.

## High-Impact Objectives

1. Fix P1 storefront presentation problems that reduce trust or product discovery.

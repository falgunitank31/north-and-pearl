# North & Pearl Promotion QA

Owner: Pareto with Tesla, Rawls, Lovelace, Gauss, Curie, and Kuhn.

## Before Any Promotion Launch

- Verify eligible products.
- Verify exclusions.
- Verify discount logic.
- Verify start/end dates and time zone.
- Verify margin floor.
- Verify cart behavior.
- Verify checkout behavior.
- Verify mobile behavior.
- Verify no conflicting discount or compare-at messaging.
- Verify customer-facing terms.
- Verify shipping and return implications.
- Verify tracking and measurement.

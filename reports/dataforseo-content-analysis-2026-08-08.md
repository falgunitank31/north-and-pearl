## Prioritized Content Edits

### P1 - Collection Intent Alignment

1. `Name Necklaces`: strengthen copy around custom name necklace, personalized name necklace, gift for her, and nameplate-style necklace language. Add a short visible FAQ answering what to check before ordering a name necklace.
2. `Initial Necklaces`: use initial necklace and initial pendant necklace as the primary topic. Add concise copy about letter selection, styling, gifting, and checking photos/options before ordering.
3. `Personalized Jewelry`: make this the umbrella page for name, initial, engraved, birthstone-inspired, and giftable personalized pieces. Add internal links to those subcollections above or directly below the grid without burying products.
4. `Jewelry Gifts for Her`: align copy with gifting search intent: birthdays, anniversaries, mom, bridesmaids, everyday thank-you gifts, and ready-to-order pieces.
5. `Birthstone Jewelry`: keep claim-safe wording as birthstone-inspired unless stones/materials are verified. Add month/gift-use guidance without claiming specific gemstones unless product facts confirm them.

### P1 - Product Page Copy Depth

- Add product-specific copy only where source facts are verified. For now, use visible-style, gifting, and option-selection guidance rather than material/performance claims.
- Prioritize pages that already have strong imagery and are internally linked from homepage/collections.
- Add 3-5 natural internal links per priority product to relevant collection and related products, avoiding duplicate boilerplate.

### P2 - Gift and Price Intent

- `Gifts Under $50` and `Gifts Under $100`: add short, shopper-first buying guidance around budget-friendly jewelry gifts, gift-ready presentation if confirmed, and choosing by recipient/occasion.
- `Birthday Jewelry Gifts` and `Anniversary Gifts`: add short occasion-specific intros and bottom copy with product-category links. Keep products above long copy.

### P2 - Homepage Content

- Add a compact text/link block that explicitly connects personalized jewelry, name necklaces, initial necklaces, jewelry gifts for her, and gifts under $100 to the correct collection paths.
- Avoid adding more long brand philosophy copy. Commercial links and product images matter more right now.

## Do Not Do Yet

- Do not create broad informational blog volume from these keywords yet.
- Do not claim sterling silver, hypoallergenic, waterproof, tarnish-free, nickel-free, or exact gemstone/material facts without supplier documentation.
- Do not request manual reindexing while the current GSC hold remains in place.

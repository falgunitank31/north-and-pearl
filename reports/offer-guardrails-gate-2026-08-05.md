# Offer Guardrails Gate - 2026-08-05

Owner: Pareto with Lead Orchestrator, Gauss, Curie, Lovelace, Rawls, Kuhn, Tesla, and Faraday.

## Decision

No bundle, discount, free-shipping threshold change, gift-with-purchase, or savings-led offer is launch-ready.

## Why

North & Pearl does not yet have verified product-by-product cost, landed cost, packaging cost, freight allocation, duty allowance, payment allowance, return/defect allowance, inspection allowance, supplier capacity, or minimum contribution-margin rules.

## Allowed Now

- Curated non-discount merchandising.
- Gift edits and collection navigation.
- Cross-sell links without savings language.
- Product recommendation sections.
- Buyer-intent landing pages and internal links.

## Blocked Now

- Bundle pricing.
- Percentage or dollar-off discounts.
- Buy-more-save-more.
- Free gift with purchase.
- Free-shipping threshold changes.
- Savings/discount claims tied to compare-at pricing.

## Next Evidence Needed

Curie and Gauss need product-level supplier/source cost evidence before Pareto can model contribution margin. Lovelace must then confirm fulfillment and return implications before any customer-facing offer is launched.

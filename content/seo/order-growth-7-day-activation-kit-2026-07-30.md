## Daily Measurement Checklist

Rawls should check:

- GA4 campaign traffic for `order_growth_july_2026`
- Sessions by `utm_source`
- Top landing pages
- Product views
- Add to cart
- Checkout starts
- Purchases
- Search Console impressions and clicks
- Shopify safe order monitor

Decision rule:

- If traffic appears but product views are weak, strengthen landing-page merchandising.
- If product views appear but add-to-cart is weak, review product images, price, product information, and trust content.
- If add-to-cart appears but checkout does not, review cart and shipping expectations.
- If no traffic appears, external distribution has not been executed or is not reaching buyers.

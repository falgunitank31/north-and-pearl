# North & Pearl Sales Strategy

Owner: Pareto.

## Immediate Objective

Generate qualified shopping sessions and move visitors toward product views, add-to-cart, checkout, and orders without damaging premium trust.

## Current Verified Baseline

- Search Console: 1 impression, 0 clicks in the latest verified monitor.
- GA4 organic: 0 rows in the latest API report.
- Shopify safe order query: 0 visible orders.
- Active catalog: 208 products.
- Purchasability: 208/208 active products available for sale.
- Cost/margin data: missing.

## Near-Term Strategy

1. Make the homepage route shoppers into buyer-intent collections.
2. Strengthen collection paths for gifts, personalized jewelry, and budget-based shopping.
3. Keep product pages claim-safe and clear.
4. Use guides and warm-audience UTM links to send traffic into commercial pages.
5. Defer discounting until contribution margin is verified.

## Top Sales Opportunities

1. Personalized routing correction: send “Shop Personalized Jewelry” traffic to the broader populated Personalized Jewelry collection.
2. Empty-cart recovery: send empty-cart shoppers into Gifts for Her, Gifts Under $100, and Personalized Jewelry instead of generic browsing.
3. Bundle architecture: create non-discount bundle concepts for necklace + earrings, gift sets, and bridesmaid buying without launching price incentives.

## Measurement

Primary metrics: product-view rate, add-to-cart rate, checkout-start rate, orders, revenue per visitor.

Secondary metrics: collection views, guide click-through, UTM campaign sessions, top product engagement.

# Product Copy Differentiation Gate - 2026-08-05

Owner: Gauss with Curie, Faraday, Pareto, Kuhn, Tesla, and Lead Orchestrator.

## Current Decision

Product-record copy differentiation may continue only inside verified factual boundaries. North & Pearl should not rewrite product records with stronger claims until the supplier facts for each product are verified product by product.

## Safe Differentiation Allowed Now

- Product type and visible style: necklace, bracelet, ring, earrings, gift box.
- Gift intent: birthday, anniversary, bridesmaid, Mother's Collection, everyday gift, where the product is already merchandised that way.
- Personalization intent when the product options or title clearly support it.
- Neutral care guidance that avoids durability/performance promises.
- Original emotional copy that does not claim material, plating, stone, allergy, waterproof, tarnish, handmade, ethical, or shipping facts.

## Blocked Until Product-Specific Evidence Exists

- Sterling silver, gold vermeil, stainless steel, solid gold, gold-plated thickness, waterproof, tarnish-free, hypoallergenic, nickel-free, lead-free, cadmium-free, handmade, ethically sourced, exact stone type, exact chain dimensions, warranty, production timeline, and supplier/manufacturer claims.
- Any product-specific FAQ answer that depends on unverified materials, sizing, personalization production method, or supplier fulfillment.

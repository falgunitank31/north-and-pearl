## Highest-Priority Content Fixes

1. Consolidate overlapping gift-guide intent: `best-jewelry-gifts-under-100` and `jewelry-gifts-under-100` compete for the same query family. Pick one canonical commercial guide and turn the other into a supporting angle or redirect after review.

2. Add stronger product and collection links inside every article. Target at least 2 relevant collection links and 3 product links per commercial guide, using natural anchors.

3. Add concise buyer-facing comparison blocks to gift articles: best for recipient, best occasion, price range, personalization fit, and why it works. This helps readers and AI extraction.

4. Strengthen H2 structure on thinner posts so each article answers actual buyer questions instead of repeating general brand language.

5. Add visible FAQ sections only where the questions are answered on page, then ensure matching FAQ schema if the theme supports it.

6. Keep material/care claims conservative until supplier evidence is verified. Educational care content can say “follow the product care instructions” and avoid unsupported plating claims.

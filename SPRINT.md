# North & Pearl Sprint 1

Sprint dates: July 27, 2026 - August 2, 2026

## Sprint Goal

Make the live storefront more premium, shoppable, and marketing-ready before scaling traffic.

## High-Impact Objectives

1. Fix P1 storefront presentation problems that reduce trust or product discovery.
2. Improve product/catalog visual confidence for active products.
3. Strengthen organic discovery paths without adding thin content.
4. Verify Merchant Center and Search Console readiness.
5. Establish the permanent measurement and documentation loop.

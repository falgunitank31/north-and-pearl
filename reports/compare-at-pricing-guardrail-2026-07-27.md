# Compare-At Pricing Guardrail

Date: 2026-07-27

## Decision

North & Pearl will not use customer-facing sale, savings, discount, urgency, or scarcity messaging from compare-at prices until the owner approves a defensible pricing or promotion policy.

## Reason

All active products currently have compare-at pricing data. Showing every product as discounted can reduce trust and make the brand feel less premium unless the pricing is backed by a clear and truthful policy.

## Current Control

- Theme presentation suppresses visible sale labels and sale-price blocks.
- Compare-at values remain in Shopify data for now.
- Marketing and SEO copy must avoid discount claims unless a verified promotion is approved.

## Validation

- Recent trust-surface and live storefront checks found compare-at data in the catalog but no visible sale badges on sampled customer-facing pages.

## Risk

- Low for current storefront because sale messaging is suppressed.
- Medium if future theme/app changes re-enable automatic sale badges without review.

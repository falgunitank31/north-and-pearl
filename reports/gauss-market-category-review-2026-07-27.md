## Category Opportunity Decision

### Priority 1: Rings

Decision: approve Wednesday sourcing research for 5-8 ring candidates, not activation.

Preferred fit:

- Personalized name rings.
- Birthstone-inspired rings.
- Stackable initial or date rings.
- Mother's/couple gift rings only if personalization options are clear.

Avoid:

- Engagement-ring positioning.
- Stone, diamond, moissanite, sterling, gold vermeil, waterproof, hypoallergenic, tarnish-free, certificate, or handmade claims unless independently verified and owner approved.
- Listings with only one usable image, heavy supplier spec-card imagery, unclear sizing, or MOQ/pricing that prevents realistic testing.

### Priority 2: Earrings

Decision: approve Wednesday sourcing research for 5-8 earring candidates, not activation.

Preferred fit:

- Initial stud earrings.
- Bow or flower earrings if visually refined and not costume-heavy.
- Statement/detail-led earrings that fit North & Pearl gifting and occasion paths.

Avoid:

- Ear health, hypoallergenic, nickel-free, sterling silver, waterproof, or gemstone claims.
- Oversized trend pieces that weaken the personalized/giftable brand center.
- Images that misrepresent scale, weight, closure, color, pair count, or included items.

### Priority 3: Gift Sets

Decision: keep gift sets as a controlled opportunity, but do not expand until Wednesday ring/earring sourcing shows clean same-product media and exact included-item evidence.

Preferred fit:

- Necklace + earrings sets.
- Initial/name gift sets.
- Bracelet + necklace sets where each included piece is visible and variants are clear.

Avoid:

- Claims about luxury packaging, ready-to-gift packaging, certificates, delivery timing, warranties, discounts, or included extras unless verified.
- Multi-piece set images that include props or alternate items not included in the sale.

### Hold: New Categories

Do not add new customer-facing categories this week. The current architecture already supports the strongest demand paths: Name Necklaces, Initial Necklaces, Birthstone Jewelry, Bracelets, Rings, Earrings, Gifts, Mother's Collection, Wedding & Bridesmaids, and Couple Jewelry.

Potential secondary landing pages for Faraday review, not catalog activation:

- Birthday Jewelry Gifts.
- Anniversary Jewelry Gifts.
- Gifts Under $50 / Gifts Under $100, only if pricing and margin are verified.

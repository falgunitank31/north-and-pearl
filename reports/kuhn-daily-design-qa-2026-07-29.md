# Kuhn Daily Design QA - 2026-07-29

## Scope

Reviewed the active catalog visual state from the latest product media-quality and live storefront QA runs.

## Findings

- Active product pages are not visually broken in automated storefront QA.
- Live storefront QA passed 208/208 active product pages.
- Cart add passed.
- Product media exists for all active products covered by live QA.
- 115 active products remain below the preferred premium media standard.
- The current acceptable-temporary image state is workable for launch hygiene, but not yet world-class jewelry brand photography.

## Design Judgment

North & Pearl can continue operating with the current product pages because product pages load, images render, and cart add works.

However, the brand should not treat the current product image library as final. The next visual-quality lift should focus on exact-source replacement images, final sample photography, consistent first-image angles, and stronger category hero imagery.

## Kuhn Next Actions

1. Prioritize visual review for products that receive early product views or add-to-cart activity once Rawls can identify those pages.
2. Replace acceptable-temporary imagery only when exact-source assets match the live product.
3. Keep collection and homepage merchandising focused on products with stronger media quality.
4. Avoid fantasy/lifestyle edits that misrepresent chain thickness, stone size, color, finish, or product scale.
